## 1. RES in every state (confidence: high)

CA ability `Monster_Karzelek_Tsereteli_Pose_LeyLineChallenge`, specials (`JPHEELFGNDA`):
- `InitialSubHurtRatio` +0.8
- `SubHurtRatio` −0.2
- `SubHurtWindRatio` −0.05

The modifiers:
- `Monster_Karzelek_Tsereteli_InitialSubHurt`:
  - It is applied when the ability starts and has no duration, so it lasts the whole fight.
  - It adds +0.8 to all 8 RES and `Actor_EndureDelta` +0.01.
- A switch on the boss's global value `_SUBHURT_NUM` picks the RES-down modifier. Its bins are [−0.5, 0.5, 1.5, 2.5, 3.5], mapped to `Karzelek_Tsereteli_NoSubHurt / SubHurt1 / SubHurt2 / SubHurt3`. `SubHurtN` = N × `SubHurtRatio` on all 7 non-Anemo RES, plus N × (`SubHurtRatio` + `SubHurtWindRatio`) on Anemo. All carry the tag `DefenseDown`.

| `_SUBHURT_NUM` | Pyro/Hydro/Dendro/Electro/Cryo/Geo/Physical | Anemo | DMG multiplier (others / Anemo) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 (initial) | 90% | 90% | 0.2174 (1/(4R+1)) / 0.2174 |
| 1 (transient only, see §2) | 70% | 65% | 0.30 / 0.35 |
| 2 | 50% | 40% | 0.50 / 0.60 |
| 3 (max) | 30% | 15% | 0.70 / 0.85 |

How the sources compare:
- [S] has columns "Initial RES" 90% all, "2 Stack RES" 50% (Anemo 40%) and "3 Stack RES" 30% (Anemo 15%). It has **no 1-stack column**.
- [W] gives `resglobal 90%` and "reduce Anemo RES by 25% and the rest by 20%, max 3 accumulations".
- Our `stygianBosses.ts` labels `initial/stacks2/stacks3` are correct. A 1-stack phase is not needed, because it is never held (§2).

No other modifier in CA changes the boss's stats: the only `MPFGPJJEDAI` property modifiers are the four above. There are no shields, invulnerability, HP thresholds or phase changes on the boss. `hpDrops` at 66% and 33% are energy drops only.

## 2. Stacks: how they are gained and lost (confidence: high on logic, medium on clamp default)

The chain of events, from CA:
1. Each exploding statue applies `GiveOwner_Subhurt_LeyLineChallenge` to the Nemesis (0.1 s). On add it does `SetGlobalValue _GO_DECREASE_SUBHURT = 1` and then fires the explosion attack (§3).
2. A switch on `_GO_DECREASE_SUBHURT` (bin 0.5–1.5) adds `Karzelek_Tsereteli_GoDereaseSubHurt`, which applies two modifiers:
   - `Monster_Karzelek_Tsereteli_SubHurtDuration`:
     - duration (`GPJONLFBEGF`) **30 s**, stacking `MultipleAllRefresh`, stack limit 3
     - on add: `AddGlobalValue _SUBHURT_NUM` **+2** (limit range on, max **3**; min omitted, so 0)
     - on remove: **−2**
   - `Monster_Karzelek_Tsereteli_ResetSubHurtMark`: 0.3 s, then sets `_GO_DECREASE_SUBHURT = 0`.

The 2 statues of one cast explode in the same frame (§3). Setting the global value to 1 twice only changes it once, and the 0.3 s mark blocks a second trigger. So **one interrupted cast adds +2 (capped at 3)**:
- 1st interrupted cast: 0 → 2 (50% / Anemo 40%)
- 2nd: → 3 (30% / Anemo 15%)
- later casts: stay at 3 and refresh the timer.

Expiry:
- `MultipleAllRefresh` refreshes every instance's 30 s timer whenever a new one is added. All instances therefore expire together **30 s after the last interrupted cast's explosion**.
- On expiry, 3 → 1 → 0 within the same frame, so RES is back to 90%.
- The next success then gives 2 stacks again, not 3.

This matches [S], which lists only Initial, 2-stack and 3-stack columns. It disagrees in wording with [S] "Grants 1 stack" and [W] "per explosion", but the resulting RES states are the same.

## 3. The sculpting cast (Fearless/Dire values; normal values in brackets)

### Cast cadence (CA `..._Pose_LeyLineChallenge`)
- On entering combat (`Karzelek_Tsereteli_Pose_AlertHandler`), `_ABILITY_Karzelek_Tsereteli_State_` is set to 1 (Pose_Normal, AI `Stage` 1).
- The first countdown, `Karzelek_Tsereteli_Pose_Normal_CountDown_LeyLineChallenge_First`, is **2 s**. When it ends, State = 2 and `_SUMMON_COUNT` = 1.
- Each later countdown, `..._CountDown_LeyLineChallenge`, is **10 s**. The normal-difficulty countdown, `UNIQUE_Karzelek_Tsereteli_Pose_Normal_CountDown`, is 15 s.
- State 2 sets AI `Stage` 2, where the only AI skill allowed is 701 `CreateIceCannon` (MON skill 701, pose 201, CD 1 s, priority 1). The boss casts it as soon as its current skill ends.
- The countdown restarts when State goes back to 1. That happens at `Karzelek_Tsereteli_CreateIceCannon_CreateEnd` (at 26.6% of `CreateIceCannon_AS`) on a finished cast, **or at the moment of a Radiance interrupt**.
- [S]: "about 8 s after entering combat it begins sculpting, and repeats every 16 s". [W]: "Summons two ice blocks 8 s after starting the battle and repeats that each 16 s since last summoning".
- **Use: first cast at 8 s, then every 16 s** ([S] = [W]). In the files, 16 s ≈ 10 s countdown + the cast up to completion or interrupt + the boss finishing its current skill + wind-up.

### Cast animation states (MON skill 701 `JEILHICOEOE`)
`CreateIceCannon_BS → BS2 → Loop → AS → Hit`. From CA `Monster_Karzelek_Tsereteli_CreateIceCannon`:
- **0–20% of BS** (`..._BSClearOldCannon` in the LeyLine pose): `_KILL_CANNON = 1`, which kills every Jack Frost still standing.
- **56.8% of BS** (`..._CreateGadget`): **2 Prime Ice blocks** (gadget 42513905).
  - It is 2 because `_ABILITY_Karzelek_Tsereteli_Abyss_` = 1, set by affix 51391. Without it the cast makes 1 block.
  - Under `LeyLineChallenge_MonsterLevelHigh` the cast also sets the animator bool `LeyLine` = true.
- **Block lifetime** (`Karzelek_Tsereteli_CreateIceCannon_IceBlock`, `lifeTime`): **6.33 s** at MonsterLevelHigh [5 s]. This is the fallback timer. Normally the blocks finish earlier, at CreateEnd or at the interrupt, through `_IceBlockKill_`.
- **The Radiance window**:
  - `Karzelek_Tsereteli_CreateIceCannon_StarModeHandler` is attached **only in `CreateIceCannon_Loop`**.
  - It is a switch on `_IS_IN_STARMODE` (bin 0.5–2.5). This is the same global value and switch type that `Monster_Fae_StarModeHandler` (affix 55316, `ConfigAbility_Monster_Fae_Common.json`) uses to show the "AffectedByStarReaction" effect, i.e. the Nemesis having Radiance: Stellar Glimmer.
  - When it triggers, `..._StarModeAction` sets `IceBlockType = 2` (animal), `IceBlockKill = 1` and State = 1, and fires the animator trigger `StarModeHit` (the cast is interrupted into `CreateIceCannon_Hit`).
- **Not interrupted**: at CreateEnd (AS 26.6%), `IceBlockKill = 1` and the type stays 1 (set at the cast's start by `..._Handler`), so the blocks become Jack Frosts.

### The blocks finish (`Karzelek_Tsereteli_CreateIceCannon_IceBlock_LifeTime`, on remove)
- **Type 1 → Big-Mouthed Jack Frost** (42513906, ability `Karzelek_Tsereteli_CreateIceCannon_Cannon`):
  - Lifetime 20 s (`..._Cannon_LifeHandler_LeyLineChallenge`) [40 s].
  - It is also killed at the next cast's start (0–20% of BS).
  - It fires every 5 s while idle (`..._Cannon_ShootHandler`, interval 5). Each shot is **120% ATK Cryo** in a 1.8 m AoE (`..._Cannon_Bullet` MonsterLevelHigh branch) [200%].
  - It has an `ElementShield` modifier; removing it (breaking the ward) kills the Jack Frost. [W] puts the ward at Cryo 80 GU; the files do not give the size.
  - **It never gives a stack or damages the boss.**
- **Type 2 → one random animal statue per block**: Hedgehog 42513907 at 1/3, otherwise Reindeer 42513908 at 1/2, otherwise SledDog 42513909 (random action, chances 0.333 then 0.5). So 2 statues per interrupted cast.
  - **The explosion delay** is `..._Animal_LifeTime_LeyLineChallenge`, **0.1 s** [elsewhere `..._Animal_LifeTime` 12 s, harmless].
  - When it ends, `GiveOwner_Subhurt_LeyLineChallenge` goes on the Nemesis and the statue is removed 0.5 s later.
  - [W]: animal ward Cryo 4 GU. [S]: the sculptures "cannot generate Superconduct stacks".
- **The explosion's damage to the Nemesis** is the attack in `GiveOwner_Subhurt_LeyLineChallenge`:
  - The fixed amount (`FGADGCECOEJ`, the field that holds `_ALL_DAMAGE` in Wavebinder's backlash and MaxHp × ratio in Moongecko's) is **499,999**.
  - No element (Physical), durability 25, Slash/Melee.
  - × 2 statues = **999,998 per interrupted cast**: 7.36% of Fearless HP, 3.08% of Dire HP.
  - [W]: "500,000 True Physical DMG twice". [S]: "1,000,000 HP worth of damage".
  - It lands at the same moment as the RES drop: the interrupt time + 0.1 s.

### Other Fearless/Dire changes (no effect on time to defeat)
These matter only for uptime (MON skills, [W]):
- IceBullet "Rapid Fire" (hovering): CD 30 s [22.5].
- IceSculpture: CD 10–10.5 s [17.5–18]; 9 shards [W].

## 4. What a team must do / model inputs

1. **Radiance must be on the Nemesis at some moment of the cast's Loop.**
   - Radiance applied earlier counts if it is still on when the Loop begins: the switch checks the current value on entry, as the Fae visual effect must.
   - So Stellar-Conduct (Radiance kept 6 s) or Stellar Swirl (8 s) needs to overlap the window that starts with the blocks appearing.
   - Model: success for cast k if a Radiance interval overlaps W_k = [T_k + lead, T_k + lead + L].
     - T_1 = 8 s, T_{k+1} = T_k + 16 s.
     - The window is bounded by the blocks' 6.33 s life. Suggested lead ≈ 1 s and L ≈ 4 s (estimates, see uncertainties), or the whole [T_k, T_k + 6.33).
   - With no timing known, for one contiguous Radiance block of length D per rotation of length P at a random phase: P(success) ≈ min(1, (D + L) / P).
2. **On a success at time t\***:
   - at t\* + 0.1 s: HP −= 999,998, and stacks = min(3, stacks + 2)
   - the stack expiry becomes t\* + 30.1 s
   - (in the files, the next cast's 10 s countdown starts at t\*)
3. **On a miss**: 2 Jack Frosts at about T_k + 6.33 s. The boss is unaffected, and stacks keep running down.
4. **DPS per state**: DPS_e(state) = DPS_e(ref) × M(RES_e(state)) / M(RES_ref), with M(R) = 1/(4R+1) for R ≥ 75%, 1 − R for 0 ≤ R < 75%, and 1 − R/2 below 0.
   - Anemo gains most: ×3.91 at 3 stacks against ×3.22 for the others.
5. **Expected Stellar-team timeline**:
   - 0–8.1 s at 90%
   - about 8.1 s: −1.0M, 50% (Anemo 40%)
   - about 24.1 s: −1.0M, 30% (Anemo 15%)
   - then −1.0M about every 16 s, with stacks held.
6. **Missing a cast**: the next chance is about 32 s after the last success, which is more than 30 s. Unless the two successes fall at different points of their windows, the stacks expire for about 2 s, at 90%, and the next success restores only 2 stacks, with 3 at the cast after.
7. **Without any Radiance**: 90% RES (×0.2174) for the whole fight, plus 2 Jack Frosts every cast.
8. Inputs this model does not use: gauge per second, reaction counts, Cryo hits, Nightsoul uptime, poise and healer. The healer could matter only for Jack Frost survival.

## 6. Uncertainties

1. **The window's length is not in the dump.**
   - It is the `CreateIceCannon_Loop` animation, whose clip length lives in Unity assets.
   - Bounds: inside the ≤ 6.33 s from the blocks appearing (56.8% of BS) to their transformation (26.6% of AS), minus the rest of BS and all of BS2.
   - Fearless/Dire's 6.33 s against 5 s, plus the `LeyLine` animator bool, suggests about 1.33 s more Loop than normal.
   - 1 s lead and 4 s length are estimates.
2. **The first cast and the cadence.**
   - The files give 2 s after the boss enters combat; the sources say about 8 s from the battle's start.
   - The files restart the 10 s countdown at the interrupt, so interrupted casts could recur sooner than finished ones (by the part of the cast not played). The sources measured about 16 s.
   - The boss also has to finish its current skill first (e.g. a 30 s-CD Rapid Fire hover), so the cadence varies by a second or more.
3. **Explosion reduction.**
   - The attack has one flag (`KPJHJIFHPID`) but lacks two (`EOCGNLFCMIA`, `JBHECHJJLMK`) that Wavebinder's backlash and Moongecko's fixed-damage attacks carry.
   - Both sources report about 500K each, though. If DEF and RES applied, it would be about 125–175K. Treated as unreduced.
4. **+2 per cast relies on two readings.**
   - It assumes `AddGlobalValue`'s omitted min is 0 and that `ONACPNFMBFG` is its value; the same type adds +1 in the Watchers.
   - If the two statues' explosions were ever more than 0.3 s apart, one cast would go straight to 3 stacks.
   - The sources' RES columns agree with the +2 reading.
5. **The meaning of `_IS_IN_STARMODE`** (the engine sets it while Radiance: Stellar Glimmer is on the enemy, values 1–2) is inferred from the Fae effect ability. Whether Radiance of any element counts is assumed (yes).
6. **Fearless and Dire both set `LeyLineChallenge_MonsterLevelHigh`.** The level scripts are not in the dump; [W] says "Fearless/Dire".
7. **Monster 25139005 for this stage** is inferred. It is the only LeyLine-pose variant, it has the LeyLine affix 5599, and the HP fits exactly.
8. **Jack Frost and animal ward sizes** (80 GU / 4 GU) come from [W] only.
9. **[W] "up to two Jack Frosts at a time, oldest removed when creating a new sculpture"**: in Fearless/Dire the files kill all of them at each new cast's start, which works out the same since each cast makes 2.
10. Dire's `LeyLineLevel` row has `HJHLKELKJLL [180]` and `TimeLimit`, probably a medal time; not read.
