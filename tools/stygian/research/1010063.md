# Secret Source Automaton: Configuration Device - Lysis Shears (Stygian 1010063)

Season 5269010 (2026-07-08 → 08-19; the wiki's season page says it ends 2026-08-11). The stage config 1010063 is used only by
LeyLineLevel 101005 (reviseLevel 104 → Lv 105, Fearless) and 101006 (reviseLevel 109 → Lv 110, Dire)
(`ExcelBinOutput/LeyLineLevelExcelConfigData.json`, `levelConfigList`). The lower difficulties use other
stage configs (the "Normal" and "Giant Claw" variants).

Monster: `DragonClaw`, id **24090206** (`ExcelBinOutput/MonsterExcelConfigData.json`: hpBase 491.5412,
GROW_CURVE_HP_2, all `*SubHurt` 0.1). It is the only DragonClaw variant whose ability list
(`BinOutput/Monster/DragonClaw_24090206.json`, key `IPFAFECPFAE`) carries
`Monster_DragonClaw_ShieldManager_LeyLineChallenge`, where 24090204/05 carry `Monster_DragonClaw_ShieldManager`. It also carries
`Monster_DragonClaw_Initial_BossRushV52` and `Monster_DragonClaw_RotateLaser`. None of its abilities branches on
difficulty (no `LeyLineChallenge_MonsterLevelHigh`), so **Fearless and Dire play the same; only HP differs**.

## 1. The opening shield ("Cold Start")
- **Shield HP: 200% of Max HP** (the Fearless or Dire HP). Source: A, `ShieldManager_CountDown` → `MEMNMMHNCPN`
  `IFLCMBKLONO: 2`, `JLMAGGHIAEE: "Electric"`, `EIOKBFMJJPE: 360`. stygian.moe: "puts up a shield equal to 200% of its
  max HP". Wiki: "special ward equal to 200% of its Max HP".
- **HP untouched while it holds:** an HP shield absorbs all DMG first. stygian.moe: "loses HP equal to 50% of the total
  damage received while shielded". No element is more or less effective in any source (the `Electric` tag is
  the shield's type; the Wavebinder's `Water` one has no element rule either).
- **RES while shielded: 110% to all 8** = base 10% + 100%. Source: A, `Monster_DragonClaw_Subhurt_InLeyLineChallenge`
  (every `Actor_*SubHurtDelta: 1`), applied by `Monster_DragonClaw_SubHurt_NormalState` (pose 201) while
  `_DEAL_SUCCESS_FOUNDATION` == 0. The base world boss has +0.5 here (60%; the wiki's "Normal" stats agree).
- **Breaking it by DMG** is out of reach: 200% Max HP at 110% RES (×0.185) is ~9.7× Max HP of DMG at 10% RES.
  If it ever broke, `DCBBKLHEHPO` sets `_MONSTER_DragonClaw_SolidPhlogiston_Center_Broken = 2`, which is the
  "both mechanisms destroyed" trigger (paralysis + 15%). Not worth modelling.
- **It breaks at the first fill** (§2). `ShieldManager_CountDown` is removed, and its `MMEPDPBJHMB` (onRemoved) runs:
  1. if `_SUM_ALL_DAMAGE` ≥ `FIGHT_PROP_MAX_HP`, set `_SUM_ALL_DAMAGE` = Max HP;
  2. an attack (`ALJBCOCLPOO`) with DMG `["_SUM_ALL_DAMAGE", 0.5, "MUL"]`, Blunt, no element (Physical), true-DMG flags set.
  - **Backlash = 0.5 × min(D, MaxHP)**: at most **50% Max HP**. D = all the DMG the shield absorbed, i.e. the team's DMG
    at 110% RES. The backlash ignores RES.
  - Wiki: "True Physical DMG equals 50% of DMG recorded by the ward, not exceeding 50% HP". stygian.moe: "50% of the
    total damage received while shielded, up to 50% of its max HP". All three agree.
- At the same moment the +100% RES goes: **RES 10% from the fill on**. The NormalState and its child RES modifier
  leave with pose 201.

## 2. Adaptive Countermeasures (the charge bar)
- **Max 200; the fill triggers at 200.** Source: A, `GHMFOIPEKHC ICKDPKAIMKM: 200`, and the `_CHARGEBAR_VALUE` monitor
  bands `[-0.5, 199.5, 200.5]` → `NyxStateMonitor_Full`, which sets the AI param `NyxState_Full` (→ the StageChange
  skill). The base world boss also has 200.
- **Passive: +2 every 1 s** (1%/s; alone, a fill at **100 s**). Source: A, modifier `Monster_DragonClaw_ShieldManager`
  `AIHECAMKEFK: 1`, `IBNPAHCLDEN: [OEIOLIMFLNP +2]`. Stygian only: the base instead starts its cannon after a 30 s
  `ShieldManager_StandbyListener` timer. This is the in-game text's "accrue progress as time passes".
- **A party member enters Nightsoul's Blessing: +5** (2.5%). Source: A, `KNFKCEBNCKC.HOIFKFGEOHO`
  `OEIOLIMFLNP: 5`. Base: 15.
- **While Nightsoul's Blessing is kept: +3 every 1 s** (1.5%/s). Source: A, `JIICKDJOGFI` `OEIOLIMFLNP: 3`,
  `OALHLCECGHM: 1`. Base: 15. This is the text's "the threshold … higher": its gains fell from 15/15 to 5/3.
  - stygian.moe: "naturally gains 1% per second … enters the Nightsoul state … 2.5% … while Nightsoul is active … 1.5%
    per second. Nightsoul states from off-field party members also count."
  - Wiki: "2 points per second … enters … 5 points … while at least a character maintains this state, 5 points per
    second instead". That is 2 + 3, so it agrees.
- Both Nightsoul gains stop when `_MONSTER_DragonClaw_ChargeFull` is set (never set in these files; a guard).
- Nothing removes progress before the fill. No other source adds any (no element, reaction, Stellar or Lunar rule).
- **The model:** P(t) = 2·⌊t⌋ + 3·(1 s ticks with ≥1 member in Nightsoul's Blessing, on- or off-field)
  + 5·(entries into Nightsoul's Blessing, any member). Fill when P ≥ 200.
  - Continuous form: T_fill ≈ (200 − 5·entries) / (2 + 3·u), with u = the share of time any member is in Nightsoul's Blessing.
  - u = 1 with 3 entries: 37 s. u = 0.5: ~51 s. No Nightsoul: 100 s.
  - Use "any member in Nightsoul's Blessing", not on-field uptime. Off-field Citlali, Xilonen, Iansan or Ororon count.
- **Exactly one fill per fight** (when the mechanisms are destroyed):
  - `Monster_DragonClaw_SubHurt_Weak` (pose 204) onAdded sets `_DEAL_SUCCESS_FOUNDATION = 1` and `_CAN_USE_FOUNDATION = 0`.
  - The shield, the charge bar and the Nightsoul gains (`ShieldManager_CountDown`) are applied only while
    `_DEAL_SUCCESS_FOUNDATION` == 0.
  - The StageChange and UltAttack skills need `_CAN_USE_FOUNDATION` (M: `CKDBHKGMDOA`).
  - Wiki: "Once the ward is broken, the boss is no longer accumulate points … during the rest of fight". stygian.moe:
    "permanently loses its shield and resistance-increase effects".

## 3. The cannon phase
1. At the fill: shield break and backlash (§1). The AI casts **StageChange** (pose 202; skill 251, priority 1). The
   in-game text: "excavate 2 blocks of solid Phlogiston".
   - The wiki says it first sweeps lasers in a square pattern. The Stygian variant adds `RotateLaser` to its states,
     but `DragonClaw_UseRotateLaser` is gated by the team GV `SGV_Monster_Level_4`.
   - The pillars (`FoundationL/R`) and then the mechanisms appear during the StageChange animation. The mechanisms
     are made at normalized time 0.78 of `StageChange` (B, `Monster_DragonClaw_UltAttack_CreateStabilizer`
     → `Stabilizer_LifeControl`). The clip length is not in the dump: **estimate ~4–6 s from fill to mechanisms**.
2. **Mechanism lifetime: 25 s.** Source: B, `DragonClaw_SolidPhlogiston_Center` modifier `Active`
   (`AIHECAMKEFK: 25` → `_SolidPhlogiston_Center_Stage = 2`, "LifeEnd").
   - At LifeEnd the mechanisms despawn and the cannon fires: `DragonClaw_UltAttack_Final`, **200% ATK Electro**
     (`NNNJILHACAF: 200`) on the whole arena. The wiki agrees ("200% ATK … across the entire arena").
   - The hit sets `Center_Broken = 3` and clears `Gathering` / `NyxState_Full`.
   - The cannon itself is timed by the `StageChange_AS` animation. Assumed to coincide with the 25 s LifeEnd.
3. **Destroying a mechanism: one hit, no HP.**
   - B, `DragonClaw_SolidPhlogiston_Center.onBeingHit_SelfCheck`: a hit by a party member within **2.3 m** of it and
     within **2 m** height sets `Stage = 1` (Break). A farther hit only plays the force-field effect.
   - The `Break` stage then adds +1 to the boss's `_MONSTER_DragonClaw_SolidPhlogiston_Center_Broken`.
   - In practice: climb each pillar (2 pillars) and land one hit. Not tied to the team's DPS. **Estimate: both broken
     ~6–10 s after they appear** (a setting; ~10–16 s after the fill in all).
   - The boss stays hittable through the charge at **10% RES**: no invulnerability or RES modifier in its Stygian states,
     but see the caveat on `SGV_Monster_Level_2` below. The on-field character spends most of that time climbing:
     model the phase as off-field damage only, or a reduced share (setting).
4. **Both broken → `UltAttack_Interrupt`** (B, monitor on `Center_Broken` band `[1.5, 2.5)` = 2):
   - **HP loss of 15% of Max HP** at once. `_SelfDamage_FinalValue = 0.15 × FIGHT_PROP_MAX_HP`, then `FEKNJLNPMGO`
     (lose HP; not DMG, so no RES). wiki and stygian.moe: 15%.
   - **Paralysis 15 s.** `Weak_Timer` `GPJONLFBEGF: 15` (sets the AI `Weak`, clears `Gathering` / `NyxState_Full`).
     wiki and stygian.moe: 15 s.
   - **Paralysis RES: −30% all** = 10% + `Monster_DragonClaw_SubHurt_Weak` (−0.4 each, pose 204; A).
5. After the paralysis: back to pose 201 at **10% RES for the rest of the fight**. No shield, no charge bar and no
   further cannon (§2). Its normal attacks only.

The failed path (the cannon fires) is not modelled. In the files `_DEAL_SUCCESS_FOUNDATION` stays 0, so on its return
to pose 201 the boss should get `ShieldManager_CountDown` again (a fresh 200% shield and 110% RES). Whether its charge
bar restarts from 0 is unknown. stygian.moe says the loss is permanent; neither site covers a failed cannon.

## 4. RES in every state (Fearless = Dire; all 8: Physical, Pyro, Hydro, Electro, Cryo, Dendro, Anemo, Geo)

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| shielded (t = 0 → fill; HP untouched) | 110% | 0.1 + 1 | Shielded 110% | shielded 110% | shielded 110 ✓ |
| cannon charge (fill → both mechanisms broken) | 10% | 0.1 | (Recovered) | 10% | recovered 10 ✓ |
| paralyzed (15 s) | −30% | 0.1 − 0.4 | Stunned −30% | Paralyzed −30% | stunned −30 ✓ |

Our table is right. The "recovered" phase starts at the fill, not after the paralysis.

**HP** (stygian.moe; a dump check): 491.5412 × curve × multiplier.
- Fearless: × 4720.888 (Lv 105) × **3.50** = 8.12 M (ours: 8,121,748).
- Dire: × 5167.530 (Lv 110) × **9.10** = 23.11 M (ours: 23,114,376).
- Both multipliers are clean (Wavebinder: 6.2 / 12.4), so the 2.85× Dire/Fearless gap is real, not a typo. The Dire figure
  is not in the downloaded chunk (it lists only Lv 105 / 8121748).

No HP thresholds. No out-of-reach period known other than StageChange's short reposition (`Rush` skill 272, poses
202/203; estimate ≤ 1–2 s).

## The fight in numbers (for the model)
```
LYSIS = {
  progressMax: 200, passivePerSec: 2, nightsoulEnter: 5, nightsoulKeepPerSec: 3,   // A ShieldManager_LeyLineChallenge
  shieldHp: 2.0 /* × Max HP */, shieldRes: 110,                                      // A ShieldManager_CountDown / Subhurt_InLeyLineChallenge
  backlash: 0.5, backlashAbsorbedCap: 1.0 /* × Max HP → ≤ 50% Max HP */, trueDmg: true,
  toMechanisms: 5 /* estimate */, mechanismLife: 25,                                  // B SolidPhlogiston_Center Active
  breakBoth: 8 /* estimate, setting */,
  hpLoss: 0.15 /* × Max HP */, stun: 15, stunRes: -30,                               // B UltAttack_Interrupt / Weak_Timer, A SubHurt_Weak
  res: 10, cycles: 1,
}
```
Timeline: [0, T_fill] shielded (DMG → D at 110%) → T_fill: −0.5·min(D, MaxHP) → [T_fill, T_fill + toMechanisms + breakBoth]
at 10% (mostly off-field DMG) → −15% Max HP → 15 s at −30% → 10% until dead.

Inputs used: DPS by element (rescaled to 110 / 10 / −30), any-member Nightsoul's Blessing uptime per second plus its
entries. Not used: gauge, hit counts, Radiance, reactions, Stellar share, healer.

Worked example: 150 K DPS at 10% RES, Nightsoul's Blessing always up, 3 entries.
- Fill at 37 s; D = 150 K × 0.2058 × 37 = 1.14 M → backlash 0.57 M.
- 10 s charge at full DPS: 1.5 M. Then −15% Max HP.
- 15 s paralysis at ×1.278: 2.88 M.
- Fearless: 8.12 M − 6.17 M left → +13 s ≈ **75 s**. Dire: −15% = 3.47 M, then 14.7 M left → +98 s ≈ **160 s**.
- Without Nightsoul's Blessing the fill alone takes 100 s.
- A shielded second is worth 0.5 × 0.2058 ≈ 10% of a second at 10% RES, so a fast fill dominates.

## Uncertainties
1. **Fill → mechanisms → both broken:** animation-bound and player-bound; only the 25 s cap is in the files. Make
   them settings: defaults 5 s + 8 s.
2. **The keep gain (+3/s):** I read it as once per tick while ≥1 member is in Nightsoul's Blessing. The wiki says "at
   least a character"; stygian.moe doesn't say whether it stacks. Each member's *entry* gives +5. The unknown
   `LPBJGJFCCBH 10` / `MAKBEMLMHAJ 25` might cap entries or set a range.
3. **`SGV_Monster_Level_1/2/4`:** team GVs that `Monster_DragonClaw_Initial_BossRushV52` (on 24090206) watches. If
   Stygian's scene set `SGV_Monster_Level_2`:
   - +300% all RES in UltAttack/StageChange states (`DragonClaw_UlAttack_SubHurt`, i.e. 310% during the charge);
   - a "MustKill" cannon.
   Level_1 means bigger pillars; Level_4 means the RotateLaser. The scene scripts aren't in the dump. Neither stygian.moe's
   RES columns nor the wiki mention any of these. The wiki's Stygian-only attacks (electrified airstrike ground,
   post-claw lasers) are the ungated BossRushV52 parts. Assumed unset.
4. **How the RES and shield leave at the fill:** child modifiers go with their parent pose-attached modifier. The files
   show no explicit removal, but all three sources agree on the outcome.
5. The shield before the boss's Alert (pose 100 → 201, ~1–2 s): assumed up from t = 0 (stygian.moe: "immediately").
6. The failed path: the shield likely comes back (see §3); not modelled.
7. The wiki's base kit says "15 points" per Nightsoul event; that is the open-world value. Its Stygian bullets match the files.
