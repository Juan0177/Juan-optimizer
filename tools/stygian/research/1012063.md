## 0. Which monster, and Fearless vs Dire

- **The monster is 25135207**, `Monster_Snegurochka_Contessa_02` (`MonsterExcelConfigData`).

  - 25135205 has `_IceShield` (the overworld variant, 500 durability, 16 s). 25135206 has `_IceShield_Abyss` (1000 durability, 16 s), probably the Lv 100 "Reprise" stage.
  - The level-to-monster link is not in the Excel tables. It is inferred from the abilities and from values that match SM. Confidence: high.
- **RES**: 25135207 has all `*SubHurt` at 0.1, Cryo included. The other variants and the overworld boss have `iceSubHurt` 0.5. W's Stygian stat block lists no Cryo RES, so it uses the 10% default. **SM agrees: all 10%.**
- **HP**:

- **Fearless and Dire share every mechanic value.** The ability has no `LeyLineChallenge_MonsterLevelHigh` branch (grep of `ConfigAbility_Monster_Snegurochka_Contessa.json` and the monster file finds no difficulty switch). Only HP and ATK change, through level and multiplier.

## 1. Fight timeline (numbers)

### Phase 1, opening: HP 100% → 81%
- **RES**: regular, all 10%.
- **Trigger at 81%.** An HP-threshold mixin (`HBNCGKIFKJH[1]`, type OJABMPOLGMO, `KKNFOGMCNOG: [0.81]`, modifiers `["Empty","EnableIceShield"]`) applies `EnableIceShield` once HP is below 81%.
- **HP floor at 80% until then.** The mixin on GV `Snegurochka_Limbo_State` maps 0 → `Limbo_On` and 1 → `Limbo_Off`. `Limbo_On` is ability state "Limbo" with `Actor_HpThresholdRatio: 0.8`.
  - `EnableIceShield` sets `Limbo_State`=1, so the floor goes away at the trigger.
  - So the crossing hit leaves HP at no less than 80%; whatever goes past 80% is lost.
  - SM: "HP initially cannot fall below 80%" and "~81%". W: "cast when her HP reached 81%. Her HP cannot drop below 80% during the first cast."
- **`EnableIceShield` on add:**
  - `Snegurochka_IceShield_Count` += 1.
  - If Count == 1, it sets the AI cooldown of skill 701 to 0.1 s (PFCEHMKMLHK, `MAILKOPBEDL:[701]`, `ONACPNFMBFG:0.1`).
  - Skill 701 ("IceShield", states IceShield_BS/Loop/AS) otherwise has a 99999 s cooldown and initial cooldown (`Monster_..._25135207.json`, skill 701). The overworld uses 10 s here, which is W's "after 10s". Stygian: at once.

### Phase 2, entry (cast of skill 701)
- **Timing**: as soon as the AI ends its current action. It blinks to its start position (arena centre) and plays `IceShield_BS`.
- **Within `IceShield_BS`, by normalized time** (DIIILKDJCNN = attach a modifier over a state's normalized-time window, `KFMBIDANKKM` start → `JHFCGHLMPGJ` end; confidence high):
  - **0 → 0.70**: `IceShield_BS_SubHurtUpUp`, all 8 RES +200% (`Actor_*SubHurtDelta: 2`), so **210% RES**.
  - **0.074 → 0.276**: `Contessa_02_Blink_Invisible`, ability state "Invincible", HitBox/HeadBox and PushCollider off (ability 24, `Monster_Snegurochka_Contessa_02_Blink`).
  - **0.698 → 0.80**: `IceShield_Stage_1` sets `Snegurochka_IceShield_Stage`=1, which starts `IceShield_Start`. The coffin and its ward are up.
- **Length: not in the dump** (clip lengths are Unity data). **Estimate ≈ 2 s** from the 81% trigger to the ward, plus 0–1 s for her current attack to end.
- **HP damage**: still possible after the trigger until the blink (Limbo is off). It is ×0.106 at 210% RES, then 0 while invincible. Model it as ≈0.

### Phase 3, coffin up (Stage 1): at most 20 s
`IceShield_Start` (on add):
- Records Max HP into `_MONSTER_Snegurochka_Contessa_IceShield_MaxHp`.
- `_SHIELD_OPEN_NUMS` += 1 (max 2).
- `_OPEN_SHIELD_STATE`=0.
- Attaches `Snegurochka_IceShield_Handler`, RigidBodyFreeze, MuteTaunt and `IceShield_CountDown`.
- **`IceShield_CountDown`**: duration `Shield_Duration` = **20 s**. On removal, if Stage is still 1, it fires the "IceShield_End" event.
- **Handler**: `_OPEN_SHIELD_STATE` 0 → `Snegurochka_IceShield` (Cryo ward); 1 → `Snegurochka_DamageShield` (the Radiance shield).

#### 3a. The Cryo ward: modifier `Snegurochka_IceShield`
- **Durability**: element `Frozen` (`JLMAGGHIAEE`), element durability `EPNEOIAINHO: "Shield_Durability"` = **1050 = 42 GU** (25 per GU). The same on both difficulties.
  - SM: "42U Cryo Shield". W: Elemental Shield Data Cryo 42.
  - The "more durable" Ley Line disturbance is 1050 vs the Abyss variant's 1000 and the overworld's 500.
- **Mixin GNLGLAPDNFL** is the ElementShield (DMG-nullifying ward) mixin. It is the same type as the Cryo Abyss Mage's ward (`ConfigAbility_Monster_Abyss_Ice.json`: 300 Frozen, `EPAFDPGENGF` 0.2, `IFLCMBKLONO` 1) and the Large Cryo Slime's (`ConfigAbility_Monster_Slime_Ice.json`: 200 Frozen, 0.2, 1). The wiki lists those two as Frozen wards with HP ratio 1 and DMG ratio 0.2.
  - `IFLCMBKLONO` = HP ratio. Here 1.
  - `EPAFDPGENGF` = DMG ratio. **Absent here**, so it takes the default, which I read as 0. **DMG does not deplete the coffin; only reactions and Blunt poise do.** Confidence: medium-high. 0.2 is written out wherever it is used, so the omitted default is not 0.2.
  - `EIOKBFMJJPE` 360 is the shield's arc angle.
  - On shield broken (`DCBBKLHEHPO`): "Broken" effect, then `Snegurochka_IceShield_Stage`=2.
- **HP is immune while the ward is up.** It is a DMG-nullifying ward (W, Ward page: "Wards that fully protect enemies from DMG (e.g. Abyss Mages)"). The coffin heals her if not broken, which fits.
- **The coffin stays targetable.** A mixin (AIBBKDNGOIB) turns off `HitBox`/`HeadBox`/`PushCollider` and turns on `HitBox_IceShield`/`PushCollider_IceShield`, so hits land on the coffin's hitbox.
- **No regeneration or decay.** There is no recovery modifier, unlike the slime's `MonsterAffix_ElementRecovery`.
- **Ward reaction coefficients**: GU of the ward removed per GU of the attacking element.
  - The Stygian coffin attaches **no reaction method**. The overworld and Abyss variants attach `Monster_Snegurochka_StarMode_IceShield` under Radiance (Electro and Anemo ×4, Pyro ×2, Geo ×2 per `BinOutput/Common/ConfigGlobalCombat.json` `ANFHKLIHDPG`; this is W's "4U per Electro/Anemo unit"). That is **not** present in the Stygian variant.
  - So the engine's standard Frozen rates apply. W, Ward page and the Gauge Data row for a Frozen 8 GU ward: Anemo 16, Geo 16, Electro 8, Pyro 4. The GF default table also has Pyro on Frozen at 0.5, which means ×2.

| Applied | Pyro | Electro | Anemo | Geo | Hydro | Cryo | Dendro | Physical |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ward GU removed per GU | **2** (Melt) | **1** (Superconduct) | **0.5** (Swirl) | **0.5** (Crystallize) | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

  - Hydro: "Hydro application does not affect the Frozen gauge on enemy wards" (W).
  - **Blunt poise**: 0.15 durability per poise point, i.e. 0.006 GU (W, Ward page, Frozen | Blunt | 0.15). Melee and Pierce poise count 0. The poise rate is not in `ConfigGlobalCombat` that I could find, so this one is W only. Reaction, damage and poise costs add up.
- **Break condition**: 1050 durability within 20 s.
  - Durability per second = 25 × (2·GU/s Pyro + 1·GU/s Electro + 0.5·GU/s Anemo + 0.5·GU/s Geo) + 0.15 × Blunt poise/s.
  - Time to break = 1050 / that rate. It breaks if that is ≤ 20 s.
  - Needs in total: 21 GU of Pyro alone, or 42 GU of Electro, or 84 GU of Anemo or Geo, or 7000 Blunt poise. Over 20 s: 1.05 / 2.1 / 4.2 GU/s, or 350 poise/s.
- **Icy storm**: while the ward is up she deals 3.125% ATK of Cryo DMG every 0.5 s around her (`IBNPAHCLDEN`, `KALPFBNJPMJ` 0.03125, think interval `AIHECAMKEFK` 0.5; W: "3.1% ATK ... each 0.5 seconds"). It matters for survival only.

#### 3b. Radiance: Stellar Glimmer: the ward becomes a shield
- **Conversion**:
  - 0.05 s after the ward is up, `Snegurochka_IceShield_Delay_CheckStar` attaches `_CheckStar`. Its mixin watches GV `_IS_IN_STARMODE` (range 0.5–2.5).
  - While the coffin is up, the first moment the boss has Radiance (at once if it already has it), `_IsInStarMode` runs:
    1. It stores the ward's remaining durability in `_MONSTER_Snegurochka_Contessa_IceShield_RemainDuability`.
    2. After 0.05 s it sets `_OPEN_SHIELD_STATE`=1, and the Handler swaps the ward for `Snegurochka_DamageShield`.
  - `_IS_IN_STARMODE` is the engine's common Radiance GV (`ConfigAbility_Monster_Fae_Common.json`, affix 55316 `Monster_Fae_StarModeHandler`).
  - Any Stellar Glimmer reaction counts, including a Stellar-Conduct off the ward's own Frozen aura.
- **Shield HP = 0.15 × Max HP × remaining / 1050.**
  - Mixin MEMNMMHNCPN has `IFLCMBKLONO: 0.15` (HP ratio of Max HP) and `HLFKFBNCCOL: [Remain, Shield_Durability, DIV]` (the share it starts with).
  - The decoding is checked twice. Wavebinder's Valiant shield uses the same mixin with `IFLCMBKLONO` 1 and `HLFKFBNCCOL` 1, and SM gives 100% Max HP. Lord of the Hidden Depths has `IFLCMBKLONO: "_Shield_Ratio"` = 0.45, and SM gives 45% HP.
  - With Radiance already on at the coffin, the ward is untouched, so the shield is **15% Max HP**: Fearless **1,550,257**, Dire **3,280,725**.
  - SM: "remaining shield percentage is converted into an HP shield worth up to 15% Max HP". W: "Barrier equals to 15% of her Max HP. This Barrier inherences the remaining percentage of Ward."
- **What damage counts**: `EPAFDPGENGF: 1` (DMG ratio 1) and no per-condition list.
  - Lord of the Hidden Depths has such a list: 3× for Lunar DMG, 1× for elemental DMG, which makes it Physical-immune.
  - So here **all DMG counts 1:1, any element, Physical included**, at her current RES: regular 10%. The shield changes no RES (unlike Wavebinder's).
  - Its element is "Ice", and it is not known whether a same-element rule applies. The Wavebinder model ignores this for its "Water" shield. Assume 1:1.
  - HP takes no damage while the shield is up (a barrier ward, W). Fav/Sac can trigger off it (SM; W, Ward page).
- **The icy storm DoT stops**: it lives on the ward modifier, which is removed (W: "DoT Cryo DMG of Ice Coffin is eliminated").
- **The 20 s `IceShield_CountDown` still runs**: the swap leaves it alone. If the shield is not broken by then, the coffin ends with the heal and no backlash. Backlash happens only on shield broken.

### Phase 4a, broken in time (ward, or the Radiance shield)
- **Shield broken (`Snegurochka_DamageShield`, `DCBBKLHEHPO`; Radiance path only)**:
  - `_SUM_ALL_DAMAGE` (everything the shield took) is capped at Max HP × 0.1 / 0.6.
  - She then hits herself, Blunt, for **0.6 × `_SUM_ALL_DAMAGE`**. That is **at most 10% Max HP in the code**, and ≈ 0.6 × shield HP ≤ **9%** when only the absorbed damage is counted.
  - SM: "up to 9% of its Max HP depending on the initially inherited HP". W: "+60% DMG dealt to the Barrier until a cap +9%".
  - There is no Stellar term, unlike Wavebinder's `_SUM_STAR_DAMAGE` × 0.9.
  - Then Stage=2.
- **Stage 2, `IceShield_Interrupted`** (both paths):
  - She hits herself for **0.1 × `_MONSTER_Snegurochka_Contessa_IceShield_MaxHp`** (Blunt, flagged as true damage; W gives the overworld 0.2 as "True Physical DMG").
  - The ice swords (gadgets 42510029–34, 42510042) are destroyed, so no counterattack.
  - The AI casts skill 801 (`SkillID` 801, then "DoSkill"): animator states Weak_BS, Weak_Loop, Weak_AS.
  - `Weak_Timer` starts: `Weak_Duration` = **10 s**, then the "Weak_End" event.
- **Backlash totals**:
  - Ward broken: **10% Max HP** (Fearless 1,033,505; Dire 2,187,150).
  - Radiance shield broken: **10% + min(0.6 × absorbed, 10%)**, ≈ 10% + 0.6 × 15% × remaining share, so **up to 19%** (or 20% if the breaking hit's overflow counts).
- **Weakened**: `Weak_State` is on while the animator is in Weak_BS or Weak_Loop (PEHKGKGGMLK).
  - It gives all 8 RES −0.5, so **all −40%, Cryo included**.
  - It lasts from just after the break until Weak_End, **≈10 s**. Weak_AS (the get-up) is not in the list, so RES is back to 10% during the get-up.
  - SM: "All RES becomes -40% for 10s". W: Stunned −40%.
  - The overworld timer is 15 s, but W says "stunned for 16 seconds". So the visible stun runs about 1 s past the RES timer.
- When `Weak_State` is removed and `_SHIELD_OPEN_NUMS` ≤ 1, `IceShield_Final_CountDown` starts.

### Phase 4b, not broken within 20 s
- `IceShield_CountDown` ends, then "IceShield_End". She plays `IceShield_AS` and fires the 6 ice swords; the sword shots sit at AS normalized 0.38–0.99. **Length unknown, estimate ≈1–2 s.**
- When the AS state ends, Stage=3, which starts `IceShield_Completed`:
  - **Heal 0.1 × Max HP** (KBICHHCOOLL `AJCGNEIGPFN` 0.1; the Abyss variant's 0.2 = W's overworld "restores 20%").
  - Skill cooldowns 201–601 are reset (she counterattacks).
  - If `_SHIELD_OPEN_NUMS` ≤ 1, `IceShield_Final_CountDown` starts.
- No weakened state; RES stays 10%.
- SM: "not destroyed within 20s, restores 10% HP". W: "HP lost and HP recovery are reduced until 10%".

### Phase 5, between coffins
- **Timer**: `IceShield_Final_CountDown`, duration **25 s**. On removal it sets skill 701's cooldown to 0.1 s, so **coffin 2 starts ≈25 s after weak ends (broken) or after the heal (not broken)**, plus the entry.
  - Broken: ≈ break + 10 + 25 + entry ≈ **break + 37 s** with a 2 s entry.
  - Not broken: ≈ ward-up + 20 + AS + 25 + entry.
  - SM and W say only "a short time after" and "up to 2 times". The 25 s is in the game files only.
- **Regular RES. No HP trigger and no floor**: Limbo stays off, and the 81% mixin can't start another coffin once Count > 1.
- **Coffin 2 is the same as phases 2–4**: 1050 Frozen, 20 s, the Radiance shield 15% × remaining, backlash 10% (+ up to 9–10%), weak 10 s or heal 10%.
- **After coffin 2** `_SHIELD_OPEN_NUMS` = 2 (an AddGV capped at 2), so there is no third coffin. Regular RES until she dies. SM: "can occur up to 2 times". W: "max of 2 times".

## 2. Model inputs and each mechanic

- **Opening**: DPS at regular RES until 19% Max HP is dealt.
- **Ward break time (no Radiance)**: `1050 / (25·(2·GUs.pyro + GUs.electro + 0.5·GUs.anemo + 0.5·GUs.geo) + 0.15·poiseBlunt/s)`, from gauge units per second with ICD counted. Hydro, Cryo, Dendro, Physical and all DMG give 0.
- **Radiance**: with Radiance uptime > 0, the ward becomes a shield at the first moment Radiance is on after ward-up.
  - Shield = 0.15 · HP · (1 − share depleted before then). A Stellar team that keeps Radiance up converts at once (+0.1 s), so the shield is the full 15%.
  - Break time = shield / DPS (all elements, regular RES 10%). The Stellar share has no special weight.
  - A Stellar-Conduct needs a Cryo aura. The Frozen ward gives one, so an Electro Stellar hit on the coffin both reacts and turns on Radiance.
  - Not broken in 20 s: heal 10%, no backlash.
- **Break, either path**: `−0.10·HP` true damage at the break, then 10 s at −40% all.
  - The Radiance path adds `min(0.6·absorbed, 0.10·HP)`. Use 0.6 × shield, ≤ 9%.
- **No HP damage** from the 81% trigger until the break or heal, apart from a sliver before the blink. Unbroken: ≈ entry + 20 s + AS.
- **Healer**: irrelevant (no team-heal mechanic).
- **Nightsoul's Blessing**: irrelevant.
- **Poise**: matters only for Blunt attacks on the Frozen ward.

## 3. Other phases, out-of-reach periods and RES changes
- **Before combat**: the BeforeCombat pose (ability 29) gives "Invincible", ghost-to-all and DenyLockOn until she enters combat. Its length is unknown, likely ≈0–1 s of the fight.
- **Combat start**: `Snegurochka_LeyLineChallenge_EnterCombat_SuperArmor`, 4 s, `Actor_EndureDelta 0` (poise only).
- **The only immune and out-of-reach windows**: the blink inside each coffin cast, and HP while the coffin is up.
- **Other RES changes**: only BS +200%, the ward and shield states (no RES change of their own), and weak −50%. No ATK or RES changes from the Radiance shield.

## 4. Uncertainties
1. **Animation lengths** are not in the dump: the entry (`IceShield_BS`, ≈2 s with her current action), the end (`IceShield_AS`, ≈1–2 s) and the weak get-up. Only normalized times are known.
2. **The ward's DMG ratio** is read as 0 because the field is absent; the Cryo Abyss Mage and slime show 0.2. Confidence medium-high. No source says DMG chips the coffin.
3. **Shield backlash cap**: the code caps the term at 10% Max HP, both sites say 9%. They agree if `_SUM_ALL_DAMAGE` holds only what was absorbed (≤ 15%). If the breaking hit's overflow counts, up to 10%.
   - It is also unclear whether `_SUM_ALL_DAMAGE` resets for coffin 2. The mixin likely resets it, as for Wavebinder's Valiants.
4. **Same-element (Cryo) DMG against the "Ice" shield**: assumed 1:1. No exclusion list in the file.
5. **Weak RES length**: the timer is 10 s from the break; the get-up has regular RES. SM says 10 s.
6. **Poise**: the Frozen Blunt rate (0.15) is from W, not found in the GF global config.
7. **Coffin 2 timing**: 25 s after weak end or heal is from the GF only (`IceShield_Final_CountDown`). SM and W give no number. `ONACPNFMBFG` 0.1 is read as the skill cooldown, and the overworld's 10 matches W's "after 10s".

## 5. Where the sources disagree
- **Cryo RES**: the dump's overworld and Abyss variants have Cryo 50%. The Stygian monster 25135207 has 10%, and SM and W's Stygian block agree. **Use 10%** (W's overworld block says 50%; that is a different monster).
- **The wiki's overworld text**: "Stellar Glimmer ... 4U per Electro/Anemo unit". That is the overworld and Abyss reaction method, **absent** in the Stygian variant, where Radiance converts the ward into the shield instead.
- **Shield backlash cap**: SM and W 9%, code 10% (see 4.3).
- **The overworld stun**: W 16 s, code timer 15 s. The animation tail is an estimate.
